⚡ The Whole Subject in 5 Lines (Click any step to jump to its visual scene) Core BCI Pipeline
1. Brain Only

A BCI lets a person control a machine using only brain signals, with no muscles.

2. Record EEG

The brain signal is usually recorded with EEG — electrodes on the scalp.

3. Clean & Filter

The signal is weak and noisy, so we clean it, filter it, and pick out features.

4. ML / DL Model

A machine learning model reads the features and decides what the user wants.

5. Command & Loop

Decision becomes a command (move wheelchair, type letter) + user sees feedback.

    Neuron Basics (86 Billion Neurons · −70 mV at rest):

    Dendrites receive signals → Cell body processes them → Axon sends action potential (spike ~1 ms) out → Synapse passes signal via chemical neurotransmitters. Millions of slow postsynaptic potentials sum up to form scalp EEG!

    Brain Lobes & Structures (Click to highlight):
    Frontal Lobe (Front · Fp, F): Thinking, planning, decisions, movement (motor cortex at back of frontal lobe under C3, Cz, C4), speech.
    Parietal Lobe (Top, behind frontal · P): Touch, body position, spatial sense. Strongest P300 at Pz.
    Temporal Lobe (Sides near ears · T): Hearing, memory, understanding language.
    Occipital Lobe (Back · O): Vision — SSVEP and Alpha rhythm with eyes closed.
    Cerebellum: Balance and coordination · Brainstem: Breathing and heartbeat.
